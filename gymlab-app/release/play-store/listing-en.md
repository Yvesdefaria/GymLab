## Full description (≤ 4,000)

```text
GymLab is your training and health hub: local-first, no account required. Your data stays on your device. No ads, no profiles, no cloud dependency.

PLAN AND ROUTINES
• A planner that builds a routine around your goal, level, days and equipment.
• 873 exercises with technique, muscles, alternatives and step-by-step guidance.
• An active program with training days, progression, deload and day swaps.

WORKOUTS
• Log weight, reps, RPE/RIR and rest.
• Rep-range PRs, estimated e1RM and load suggestions.
• Warm-ups, supersets, a rest timer and local notifications.

REAL PROGRESS
• Volume, frequency, strength and per-exercise trends.
• Body weight, measurements, composition and progress photos.
• Streaks, achievements, session comparison and full history.

HEALTH AND NUTRITION
• Steps from Health Connect, plus a home-screen widget.
• BMI, calories and macros, water, 1RM, body fat and measurements.
• Nutrition and supplements, with honest ranges and disclaimers.

PRIVACY BY DESIGN
• Workout and health data are stored on your device.
• Optional telemetry, off by default and switchable in Settings.
• Export a full backup or erase everything from Settings.
• No account, no ads and no data selling.

GymLab is not a medical device. The information is educational and does not replace advice from a healthcare professional.
```
